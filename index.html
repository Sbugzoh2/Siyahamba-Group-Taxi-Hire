        How it works

        1. The group requestsOne person books for everyone, picks a short or long trip, and sees the price for the whole group and per person.
        2. A checked driver acceptsEvery driver's ID, licence, PrDP and vehicle are checked by Siyahamba. You see their photo, their taxi and its number plate.
        3. Pay safely, ride togetherPay in the app and Siyahamba holds the money until you confirm you've arrived. Or pay the driver directly.
