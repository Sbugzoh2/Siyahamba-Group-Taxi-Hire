Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Siyahamba Group Taxi Hire ("Siyahamba", "we") helps groups hire a whole minibus taxi, Sprinter or bus. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the Siyahamba website and Android app, why we need it, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who is responsible

The responsible party is Malita (Pty) Ltd, Shemula Area, Ingwavuma, KwaZulu-Natal, 3968, South Africa. Our Information Officer is Sibusiso Gumede, who you can reach at sbugzoh2@gmail.com or +27 83 212 0333.

What we collect

WhoInformationWhy
Everyone with an accountName, phone number, email address, sign-in details (Google or email and password)To run your account on any phone, contact you about trips, and email receipts
PassengersPick-up and destination addresses, trip dates, group size, emergency contact (optional), wallet balance and paymentsTo price and arrange trips, hold your payment safely until the trip is done, and help in an emergency
DriversID or passport number, nationality, ID copy, driver's licence, PrDP, profile photo, vehicle photo and registration, race (optional), driver credit and earningsTo check that every driver is who they say they are and is allowed to carry passengers, which keeps passengers safe
During a tripThe driver's live location, and the passenger's if they choose to share it; messages, ratings and reports of problemsTo show the taxi on the map, let you share your trip with family, handle SOS alerts and sort out complaints

Race is optional and only collected with your consent, for transport-sector transformation reporting. You can leave it blank.

Location

The app uses your phone's location only when you allow it: to find your pick-up spot, and for drivers to show where the taxi is during a trip. Location sharing stops when the trip ends. You can turn location off in your phone's settings at any time.

Who we share it with

  • The other person on your trip. Passengers see the driver's name, photo, vehicle, number plate, phone and rating. Drivers see the booking person's name, phone, and pick-up and drop-off addresses.
  • People you share a trip with. Only if you use "Share trip".
  • Our service providers. Google Firebase stores the app's data and handles sign-in. OpenStreetMap, and Google Maps if switched on, show maps and work out distances. Our email service sends receipts. These providers may store data outside South Africa, under agreements that protect it as POPIA requires.
  • The authorities. Only when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, such as after an SOS alert.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and driver documents: while your account is open, and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete your account.
  • Trip and payment records: 5 years, as tax and financial laws require. After your account is deleted these records are no longer linked to your contact details.
  • Live location: only while a trip is in progress.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account (or tap Delete my account at the bottom of any screen), choose Delete my account and confirm. You can also email sbugzoh2@gmail.com from the address on your account.

We then delete your profile, contact details, wallet, driver profile and uploaded documents within 30 days, and email you when it's done. If you have money in your wallet or driver credit, we pay it back to you first. Trips that are still in progress must finish before we can delete the account.

Keeping it safe

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Security rules make sure that only you, and our owners where needed, can see your private details, documents and money records. If we ever learn of a data breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact our Information Officer. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za, enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account. Children may travel in a group booked by an adult, but we don't collect their details.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
